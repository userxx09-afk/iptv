Tapper Alexa relay

Voice control for Tapper IPTV and Gary's TV.

Enter the setup PIN

The installer showed a six-digit PIN. You can also get it on this computer with tapper-alexa-relay pin (Ubuntu: sudo tapper-alexa-relay pin --data /var/lib/tapper-alexa-relay).

1 Public HTTPS address

Alexa calls this relay over HTTPS from the internet. With Cloudflare: Zero Trust › Networks › Tunnels › your tunnel › Public Hostname › add alexa.tapper.co → Service HTTP, URL localhost:8787. Then enter the address here and check it.

2 Let this relay use your Amazon account (once)

Amazon only lets programs sign in with a "security profile" you make yourself. It takes two minutes and is free:

  1. Open Login with Amazon and sign in with the same Amazon account your Echos use. If it asks, finish the free developer registration.
  2. Press Create a New Security Profile. Name: Tapper Alexa relay, description: Sets up my TV skill, privacy URL: https://tapper.co. Save.
  3. Open the profile's Web Settings › Edit › Allowed Return URLs: add and save.
  4. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret from Web Settings into the boxes below.

3 Sign in with Amazon

Signs in and lets this relay create and manage your private Alexa skill. Nothing is published.

4 Create the Alexa skill

The relay makes a private skill on your account, points it here and turns it on for every Echo signed in to that account.


    


    

      
5 Test it

      
Types a phrase into Alexa's simulator, as if you'd said it to an Echo. Before a TV is linked, Alexa should say the TV isn't linked yet: that means the whole path works.

      

      

    


    

      
6 Link your TVs

      
Tapper IPTV (on each Fire TV): Settings › Alexa › Set relay address , Relay key , then Link to Alexa.

      
Gary's TV (once, on the GarysTV computer): Settings › Alexa › relay address  and key . Then on each TV: Settings › Alexa › Link.

      
The TV shows a code. Say "Alexa, ask tapper to link code 123 456". Then: "play ESPN", "channel 204", "next channel", "pause", "open the guide", "show my recordings". Several TVs can be linked to one Alexa account; Alexa controls the one that has the app open.
